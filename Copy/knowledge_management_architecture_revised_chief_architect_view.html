Knowledge Management Architecture View

Revised senior architecture view showing the target operating model for knowledge management. The primary lane shows the governed enterprise knowledge flow. The Group AI lane shows the reusable AI capabilities integrated at specific control points. Data Fabric is positioned as the primary governed interface for published knowledge products and managed enterprise access.

Chief architect reading of the target model

This architecture is intentionally not a restatement of the reference architecture. It is a use-case-oriented operating view. The primary lane shows the governed knowledge lifecycle as it applies to knowledge management. The lower lane shows where Group AI products are consumed into that lifecycle. The result is a joint architecture where responsibilities remain clear, but the end-to-end consumer outcome is also visible.

Data Platform responsibility Governed ingestion, preparation, semantic shaping, persistence, publication and lifecycle management of enterprise knowledge assets.
Group AI responsibility Provision of reusable AI capabilities including extraction, embedding and indexing services, model access, moderation, agent hosting and MCP-aligned integration.
Why this corrected view is stronger It is grounded in real consumer demand, shows clear integration points for Group AI, and avoids reading as a simple copy of the reference architecture.
